## M1. XDM background (what we start from)
The exchange-hole dipole moment (XDM) model [Becke–Johnson 2005, 2007] builds the
dispersion energy from nonempirical pairwise coefficients,
  E_disp = −Σ_{i<j} Σ_{n=6,8,10} C_{n,ij} / (R_vdw,ij^n + R_ij^n),
with Becke–Johnson damping R_vdw,ij = a1·R_c,ij + a2 (R_c from the C8/C6, C10/C6, C10/C8
ratios) and two fitted parameters (a1, a2). The C_n are assembled from atomic
polarizabilities α_i and exchange-hole multipole moments ⟨M_ℓ²⟩_i, e.g.
  C6,ij = α_i α_j ⟨M1²⟩_i⟨M1²⟩_j / (⟨M1²⟩_i α_j + ⟨M1²⟩_j α_i).
Atomic quantities come from a **Hirshfeld partition** of the molecular/crystal density;
the atomic polarizability is scaled from a free-atom reference by the effective volume,
  α_i = (V_i^AIM / V_i^free) · α_i^free,
conventionally with **neutral** free-atom references and, in the periodic implementation,
a cap min(V^AIM/V^free, 1) [Otero-de-la-Roza–Johnson 2012, 2013].

## M2. The problem for ions
For ionic systems the neutral Hirshfeld partition gives unphysical near-zero atomic
charges, and the neutral free-atom reference mis-scales ionic polarizabilities: a cation
has lost a whole valence shell, so no volume ratio applied to the neutral α recovers its
(much smaller) polarizability; anions are conversely under-polarized and the cap forbids
V^AIM/V^free > 1. This is the same failure the TS lineage identified [Tkatchenko–Scheffler
2009; Bučko 2013/2014; Gould FI 2016].

## M3. Charge-aware references (this work)
We (i) partition with **iterative Hirshfeld (Hirshfeld-I)** [Bultinck 2007], giving
physical fractional atomic charges Q_i and charge-matched effective volumes; (ii) **drop
the cap**; and (iii) replace the neutral polarizability reference with a charge-aware one
via **five** selectable "routes" (plus the neutral baseline):

- **neutral** (baseline): unchanged XDM, α_i = (V^AIM/V^free)·α^free(neutral).
- **gould** (FI-faithful): α_i = α^FI(Q_i) · V^AIM / V^free(0), where α^FI(Q) is the
  free-ion static polarizability from the Gould–Bučko database [2016], linearly
  interpolated in electron number between bracketing integer charge states. Uses the
  neutral free-atom volume in the denominator (the Gould α is ~frozen-orbital-sized, so
  the neutral volume is the consistent partner).
- **scale**: α_i = α^free(0) · (V^AIM/V^free)^{p′_Z} with element-specific exponents
  p′_Z = p_Z − 0.615 [Gould 2016], generalizing the neutral p′=1. (Note: FI itself uses
  linear scaling; scale is a distinct, more aggressive hypothesis.)
- **compute**: α^free(Q) = α^free(0)·[⟨r²⟩²/N]_Q / [⟨r²⟩²/N]_0, a Kirkwood estimator
  evaluated on the confined ld1.x ion density at charge Q (calibrated to the known neutral
  free-atom α so the prefactor cancels), then the FI volume factor V^AIM/V^free(Q). Handles
  any charge (works on the bound confined density, not a linear response), but **overestimates
  soft anions** (S²⁻ ≈ 206 a₀³).
- **stern**: α^free(Q) = α^free(0)·r_stern(Z,Q), r_stern from our confined-ion
  uncoupled Sternheimer/coupled-perturbed-KS solver on ld1.x ions (the box makes the response
  finite; the uncoupled absolute error cancels in the ratio, verified vs H α=4.5). Cations
  extend to **+2** (r_stern_p2; validated: α(Mg²⁺) 0.31, α(Ca²⁺) 2.6 a₀³, vs an unphysical ≈20
  from linear +1 extrapolation). Negative side is limited to −1: a free double anion is unbound,
  so its uncoupled response diverges — deep anions are handled by `sternws`.
- **sternws** (Watson-sphere self-consistent Sternheimer; the deep-anion route): the divergence
  above is a *vacuum* artifact — in a crystal the anion is bound by the Madelung field. We model
  that field with a **Watson sphere** (a compensating shell of charge −Q at radius
  R_W = ⟨r³⟩^(1/3) of the neutral, following Bučko's TS/HI rule) placed **inside** a
  self-consistent radial KS(LDA) atomic SCF; the sphere binds O²⁻/S²⁻/N³⁻ so the density *and*
  the (Pauli-projected) Sternheimer response stay finite. For **cations/neutral** sternws reuses
  the bound stern ratios (renormalized to α^free); for **anions** it uses the **raw** Watson-confined
  α directly (a₀³), since the neutral-based renormalization is not transferable across shell-filling
  (it pulls the deep anion below experiment) whereas the raw Watson α already matches the in-crystal
  value. Unphysical over-fills (an electron forced into the next shell, e.g. F²⁻/S³⁻) fall back to
  neutral scaling.

Reference **densities** for the Hirshfeld-I fractional/ionic pro-atoms are generated by
confinement (free anions being unbound at semilocal DFT): either Gaussian-basis
confinement (Psi4 PBE/PBE0/def2-TZVP, collocated + spherically averaged) or confined
numerical ld1.x (box rmax = 3.6·R99(neutral), stepped down if unbound), the latter
consistent with critic2's shipped neutral/cation set. Whole periodic table, q=−1 (+ q=−2
p-block). For the **sternws** anion references the confinement is the Watson sphere (soft
Coulombic, R_W-sized) inside a self-contained radial LDA SCF, which — unlike a hard box —
keeps the linear *response* well-behaved. Exchange-hole moments ⟨M_ℓ²⟩ are re-partitioned with
the same HI weights.

**Reference-α validation (in-crystal, Tessman–Kahn–Shockley 1953):** sternws is the only route
that gives a stable, first-principles deep-anion α matching experiment — S²⁻ 4.66 Å³ (Tessman
4.8–5.9), O²⁻ 1.37 Å³ (MgO, Tessman 1.65); `compute` (30 Å³) is far too soft and `stern`'s −1
clamp (6.7 Å³) too stiff. The independent Watson SCF also reproduces the stern cation table
(Mg²⁺, Ca²⁺ ratios to a few %), cross-validating both.

## M4. Damping parameters (transferable, not refit per charge-route)
The BJ parameters (a1, a2) are fit once on the KB49 benchmark [Kannemann–Becke 2010;
Otero-de-la-Roza–Johnson 2013] by minimizing the relative-percent deviation (RMSP) of
interaction energies (coefficients from critic2's XDM output; E_disp(a1,a2) is closed-form
→ 2-parameter least-squares). KB49 is **neutral, covalent/dispersion-bound** dimers, where
the charge-aware α ≈ neutral, so a single set of a1/a2 serves all routes — the ionic solids
are the *test* set, not the *fit* set. This is the literature precedent [TS+HI reuses TS
damping; FI keeps β=0.83] and we confirm it (fitted values sit near the neutral ones). The
charge-aware routes — including **sternws** — therefore use the **same a1/a2 as neutral/stern**;
the sternws-vs-stern (and vs-neutral) cohesive-energy comparison **at fixed damping** is the
deliverable, so any change reflects the reference-α physics rather than a re-tuned cutoff. A
joint refit on a set that includes ionic solids is reported only as a robustness check (it
risks overfitting and breaks the transferable-damping philosophy).

## M5. Computational details
- **Molecular:** Psi4 PBE/def2-TZVP → fchk → critic2 `xdm_wfn` on a Becke/Franchini mesh
  (cusp-safe). Interaction energy E_int = [E_DFT + E_disp](complex) − Σ monomers.
- **Periodic:** Quantum ESPRESSO 7.2 PBE-PAW (kjpaw_psl 1.0.0), ecutwfc/ecutrho as
  converged; pp.x valence density (plot_num=0) + ELF (plot_num=8) → critic2 `xdm grid`
  with ZPSP core reconstruction (HI integrates the smooth valence density; Q = ZPSP −
  ∫w·ρ_val). Cohesive energy vs spin-polarized free-atom PBE references.
- **KB49 QE variant (packaged):** fixed large MT-isolated cubic box, ecutwfc=80/ecutrho=800,
  same PAW set; needs a large-memory HPC node.

## M6. What is novel vs prior art
TS+HI [Bučko] already uses HI volumes but keeps **neutral** reference α (charge enters
only through the volume ratio). We additionally **replace the reference α** (gould/scale/
compute/stern/sternws) and apply it within **XDM** (which, unlike TS, includes C8/C10 and
exchange-hole moments). The confined-ion Sternheimer route has no direct literature
equivalent; and **sternws** — a self-consistent Watson-sphere atom feeding a Pauli-projected
Sternheimer response — is, to our knowledge, the first first-principles deep-anion (−2/−3)
dispersion reference that stays finite and matches in-crystal α (Tessman), where every
volume-scaling or clamp surrogate fails.
